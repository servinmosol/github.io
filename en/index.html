FAQ

Everything clear before we start.

Absolute transparency. We solve your doubts before writing a single line of code.

Is my data secure? ↓

Your data is absolutely protected. We work exclusively with private enterprise APIs that strictly comply with GDPR and European regulations (zero retention). Your information is NEVER used to train public AI models.

Do I have to change my current CRM? ↓

You don't have to change your workflow at all. We adapt to your current infrastructure and connect directly to your tools (CRM, ERP, web) via API, ensuring a smooth integration with no learning curve for your team.

Who owns the code? ↓

The development belongs 100% to you. Upon project completion, we hand over all source code, system architecture, and access credentials. You are the absolute owner of your tool, with no technical lock-ins or dependencies on any provider (Vendor Lock-in).

Are there mandatory monthly fees? ↓

There are no mandatory monthly fees for our software. Our model is a one-time payment for development. From then on, you only assume the direct cost at the provider's price for your own server or minimum API consumption (usually a few cents a month), with no hidden margins from us.
